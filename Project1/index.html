The Launch Site

As a fortress and a research station, Der Eisendrache has spent 600 years overlooking the Salzach Valley. The last 80 of those years have also been spent pointing into the stars. Get ready for the countdown.

About the Destination

Three things keep travellers coming up the gondola to the doorstep of this massive fortress.

The Castle

The Gothic 14th-century architecture, along with the various courtyards, is encapsulating. If you search around long enough you may find four paintings scattered across the castle grounds that tell the tragic tale of the mythical Wolf King.

The Rocket Launch Sites

With the undercroft turned into a test facility for Group 935 in 1943, visitors can tour the various facilities — and even Ground Zero, where the rockets changed the course of history.

The History

The fortress has hosted many sieges. What makes Der Eisendrache known across the globe, though, is the infamous infiltration of November 5th, 1945. Four soldiers — Tank Dempsey, Edward Richtofen, Takeo Masaki and Nikolai Belinski — made their way up the castle, shot down an unknown entity sent to the moon’s Griffin Station, and launched every remaining rocket at the moon, eviscerating it.
